ABOUT SEORINNE

A quieter
point of view.

SEORINNE is a skincare collection built around light, texture and familiar forms. Clear glass, white closures and simple lettering give the range a shared visual language.

The collection brings together twelve formats, from a gel cleanser to a hand cream. This site introduces their textures, sizes and packaging, and offers a place to begin a business conversation.

Explore products

Three guiding principles.

  1. 01

    Familiar forms

    Bottles, jars and tubes give each format a clear place in the collection.

  2. 02

    Clear presentation

    Names, types and sizes sit together, with space to read each detail.

  3. 03

    A shared language

    A restrained palette and consistent lettering connect the range.
