Privacy, considered.

What we collect

When you send a business inquiry, we collect your name, company, work email, country or region, and your message. We do not offer accounts, online purchases or newsletters on this site.

How we use it

Your inquiry is stored securely so our team can read it and reply by email. A notification about the new inquiry is sent to our team's business mailbox. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Retention and your rights

We keep inquiries only as long as needed to respond and follow up. You can ask us to access or delete your inquiry by writing to us through the inquiry form. The exact retention period and contact for privacy requests will be published here before launch.
